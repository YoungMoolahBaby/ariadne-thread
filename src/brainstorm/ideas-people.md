# Anchored in other people

People are the oldest thread. A person outside the simulation sees your real body, is not fooled by the content, and can act. Every serious practice of going somewhere strange, from caving to psychedelic therapy to shamanic journeying, keeps someone at the other end (see [minds coming back](../elsewhere/minds-coming-back.md)).

## O1. The anchor person

*Job: extract, verify · Restores: everything · Today: yes*

Someone outside who knows you are in, watches your real body, can call you back, and can confirm, once you are out, that you are out. A trip sitter, *The Matrix*'s operator, Ariadne at the door.

- **Why:** the only thread that works against every threat on the list, including some of a dishonest platform: they can lift the headset or pull the plug.
- **Protects against:** everything, as long as they are present and attentive.
- **Weak spot:** needs a person. Most VR is used alone. Their "you're out" message reaches you through your senses, like everything else.
- **Build it:** a lightweight version is a "spectator" view on a phone with a big "call them back" button that triggers the platform exit. A remote anchor person is possible but weaker.

## O2. The call-back signal

*Job: extract · Restores: layer, time · Today: yes*

An agreed signal that means one thing only: "come back now". A specific touch on the shoulder, a sound, a phrase. Rehearsed so that it is recognised instantly, and delivered on a channel the content does not control.

- **Why:** shamanic practitioners use a changed drum rhythm to call the journeyer back; *Inception* uses a piece of music. Without an agreed signal, outside interruptions are just noise the brain tries to fit into the story.
- **Protects against:** drift, own mind; works best paired with O1.
- **Weak spot:** a signal heard through the headset's headphones can be imitated by content; touch is harder to fake.
- **Build it:** today, with nothing but agreement and practice.

## O3. The buddy line

*Job: guide, extract · Restores: layer, place, time · Today: buildable*

Two people go in together, each holding the other's thread: each can see the other's depth, time in and real-world status, and can trigger the other's exit. Cave divers dive in pairs and plan for the buddy's air as well as their own.

- **Why:** it works when there is no one outside. Each person is outside the other's absorption.
- **Protects against:** drift, captivation, careless content.
- **Weak spot:** both can drift together; shared worlds may absorb both equally.
- **Build it:** a shared session status and a "pull my partner out" control in a multiplayer app.
