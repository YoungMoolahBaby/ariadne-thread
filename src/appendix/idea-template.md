# Idea card template

Copy this into the right `src/brainstorm/ideas-*.md` page, give it the next free ID for that group (H, P, O or Y), add it to the table in `src/brainstorm/ideas.md`, and add a row to `src/brainstorm/comparison.md`.

```markdown
## X0. Name of the idea

*Job: guide | verify | extract · Restores: place, layer, self, time, state · Today: yes | partly | buildable | no*

One paragraph: what it is, concretely enough that someone could sketch it.

- **Why:** the reasoning, and where the idea comes from (research, another field, fiction).
- **Protects against:** which of drift, careless, captivating, hostile, own mind.
- **Weak spot:** how it fails, and who could defeat it.
- **Build it:** what a first prototype would be.
```

## Field guide

- **Job**: *guide* helps you find the way; *verify* helps you know you are back; *extract* brings you out whether or not you find the way. See [requirements](../brainstorm/requirements.md#three-jobs).
- **Restores**: which of the [five starting points](../problem/starting-points.md) it brings back.
- **Protects against**: the [five threats](../problem/threat-models.md).
- **Anchor group**: H hardware, P platform, O other people, Y you. An idea anchored only in the content is not a thread (it is bread); note it under the closest group with that caveat, or not at all.
